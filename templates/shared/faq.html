Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for an event?

Browse events, open the one you want, and click Register. If it's full, you'll be added to the waitlist and confirmed automatically if a seat opens up.

Where's my ticket?

Go to My Registrations and open any confirmed event to see your QR ticket and printable receipt.

Can I cancel my registration?

Yes -- open the event from My Registrations and click Cancel. If someone is on the waitlist, they'll automatically take your seat.

How do organizers publish events?

Organizers create an event as a draft, then submit it for approval. An admin reviews and either approves (making it visible to students) or rejects it.

How do I get my certificate?

Certificates are available after an organizer marks you as attended (either manually or by scanning your ticket's QR code at the event).
