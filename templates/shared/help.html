Help & Support

Contact an organizer

Every event's detail page shows the organizer's name. For questions about a specific event, reach out to them directly through your college's usual channels.

Account issues

If you're locked out or your account was blocked in error, contact your system administrator -- they can unblock your account or reset your password from the Admin panel.

Technical problems

If something looks broken (a page not loading, a QR code not scanning), try refreshing first. If it persists, note what you were doing and reach out to the portal administrator.
